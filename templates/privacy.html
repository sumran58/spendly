Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, as well as data generated through your use of the app:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, and password
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps and session activity

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve the Service. This includes:

  • Authenticating your account and keeping it secure
  • Displaying your expense records, summaries, and category breakdowns
  • Diagnosing bugs and improving the reliability of the app

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with the Service and are retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures, such as password hashing, to protect your information. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. Where the Service relies on third-party infrastructure (for example, hosting or font providers), those providers may process limited technical data, such as IP addresses, solely to deliver their service to us. We do not permit these providers to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website, or via your account administrator.
